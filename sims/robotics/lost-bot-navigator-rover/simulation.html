Lost Bot Navigator — Obstacle Escape Simulator

  1. Press ▶ Start — the rover drives itself toward the rescue zone. No remote control here.
  2. Watch the straight laser beam in front of the rover — it stops where it hits an obstacle or a wall. The Obstacle Distance meter shows what it currently reads, with the Caution and Danger lines marked on it.
  3. The walls of the arena count as obstacles too — the rover slows, stops and turns away from them the same way.
  4. Drag any obstacle to move it closer or farther — watch the rover's decision change the moment the reading crosses a threshold.
  5. Push an obstacle into the danger zone and watch the full recovery: Stop → Reverse → Turn → Continue, not an instant snap.
  6. Tune Movement Speed (normal cruising speed) and Slow Speed (how fast it creeps in the caution zone), plus Caution and Danger Distance.
  7. Click an obstacle to select it. Use Add / Remove to change the obstacles, and Make Moving to let it wander around. Low / High change how fast all moving obstacles travel.
  8. Switch Path Difficulty for a preset layout, or press Random Obstacle Set for 1–7 random static and moving obstacles.
  9. Drag the dashed rescue zone anywhere. The mission completes once the rover is completely inside it — moving obstacles freeze too. Press ↺ Reset Simulation to try again.
  10. Pick a Simulation Mode: Sensor Test (rover parked — drag the obstacle and watch the reading), Speed Control (fast → slow → stop), Obstacle Recovery (stop → reverse → turn), Path Challenge and Full Rescue Mission (timed, with a result screen).
  11. Use Fault Injection to break a part of the rover — sensor, motor driver, or one of the responses — and watch the warnings and what goes wrong.
  12. The strip under the map follows each decision: Sensor → Zone → Decision → Motors. The Motor Driver panel shows each motor's direction and power.
  13. Switch Display Mode to Simplified for the plain version without these extras.
