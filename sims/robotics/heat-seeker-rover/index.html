HEAT SEEKER ROVER

  1. Press ▶ Start to release the rover — it waits armed until you do. ⏸ Pause and ↺ Reset are always there under the map.
  2. Watch the rover's head sweep left, forward and right. The What the Rover Feels bars show what the flame sensor reads — longer and brighter means a stronger heat signature.
  3. Drag the 🔥 flame anywhere on the map, or press Move Flame, and watch the rover change its mind about where to go.
  4. Click any empty square to build a wall, click a wall to remove it, or press Clear Walls. The ultrasonic sensor watches for them ahead.
  5. Drag the rover itself to drop it somewhere new — it re-scans from wherever you leave it.
  6. Tune the Mission Controls: Heat Intensity, Heat Threshold, Flame Sensitivity, Ultrasonic Range, Rover Speed and Scan Spread.
  7. Check the Ultrasonic distance meter and the Path Clear / Obstacle Detected badge, then read Rover is… to see the decision it just made.
  8. Try setting Heat Threshold above Heat Intensity — the rover can never feel enough heat to stop. Lower it and the rover holds when it gets close.
