Bluetooth Navigator

  1. Press Start Pairing — the remote searches for nearby Bluetooth devices.
  2. When the rover appears in the device list, tap it to send a pairing request.
  3. Once Connected, the D-pad lights up. Press Forward, Backward, Left, Right, or Stop and watch the command travel across the signal link before the rover responds.
  4. Each command keeps the rover moving that way until you send a new one — just like a real RC command, it doesn't stop on its own.
  5. Pick a colour and press Add Drop Zone to place a drop zone of that colour. Add as many as you need.
  6. Drive the rover into the loading area, pick a cargo colour and press Load Object — repeat to load as many parcels as you want. Loaded parcels leave the loading area until you reset.
  7. Drive into a coloured drop zone, pick the colour to drop and press Unload Object. Matching colours are marked ✓ correct, mismatches ✕ wrong — dropped parcels stay in the zone.
  8. Drag the loading area or any drop zone on the map to move it. Select a drop zone and press Remove Zone to delete it.
  9. Try Disconnect mid-drive — the D-pad disables and commands stop reaching the rover. Press Reconnect to pair again and pick up where you left off.
