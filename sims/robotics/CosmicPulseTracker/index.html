COSMIC PULSE TRACKER — LOST SIGNAL LAB

  1. Gibson is lost somewhere in the dark zone. His beacon is transmitting — pick what he is sending: SOS, Warning, Location Pulse or System Glitch.
  2. Choose an RF pulse pattern. Long and doubled pulses survive damage; short and continuous ones fall apart.
  3. Click or drag anywhere in the dark zone to move the receiver. Drag the white knob on the antenna to aim it.
  4. Watch the OLED climb NO SIGNAL → FAR → NEARBY → GIBSON IS HERE! and the buzzer beep faster as you close in.
  5. Push up Signal Damage and Background Interference and watch the decoder start reading the wrong message.
  6. Watch Decode Confidence on the right — it is the share of symbols that survived the trip, and it is what decides whether the OLED shows a locked message, a garbled one, or nothing.
