STARLIGHT DECODER LAB

Spectral colour sensing & star classification

  1. Pick a wand colour. The tip lights up, but nothing is read yet — the sensor only sees light once you start the scan.
  2. Set distance, brightness, and interference however you like, then press Start. The astronaut swishes the wand through the air on her own and points it at the decoder's sensor lens.
  3. Watch the AS7341 channel bars ramp up as the sensor reads — the tallest bars are what the controller compares against its colour map.
  4. Move the wand far away or dim it right down and scan again — the reading comes back too weak to classify.
  5. Turn interference up and scan a colour you already know — watch a clean reading turn into a mixed, unclear one.
  6. Use Run Guided Decode to watch all five colours scan back-to-back and fill in the constellation.
