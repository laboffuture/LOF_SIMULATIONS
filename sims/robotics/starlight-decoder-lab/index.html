STARLIGHT DECODER LAB

Spectral colour sensing & star classification

  1. Pick a wand colour. The tip lights up, but nothing is read yet — the sensor only sees light once you start the scan.
  2. Set LED Intensity and interference however you like, then press Start. The wand swishes through the air on its own and points itself at the decoder's sensor lens.
  3. Watch the decoder console's AS7341 channel graph plot F1-F8 once a star is identified — the tallest points are what the controller compares against its colour map. 100% intensity shows the full reference reading; dim it down and the graph dims with it, right down to a small sensor noise floor instead of dropping to zero.
  4. Dim the LED Intensity right down and scan again — the reading comes back too weak to classify.
  5. Turn interference up and scan a colour you already know — watch a clean reading turn into a mixed, unclear one.
  6. Use Run Guided Decode to watch all six colours scan back-to-back and fill in the constellation.

Star Mapping Reference

Blue-dominant channels → Rigel (Blue Star)
Saturated blue-white → Sirius (Sky Blue Star)
Balanced, bright channels → Procyon (White Star)
Yellow-warm dominant → the Sun (Yellow Star)
Orange-warm dominant → Arcturus (Orange Star)
Red-dominant channels → Betelgeuse (Red Star)
Sirius and Procyon read almost identically to the AS7341 — both are bright enough to saturate most channels, a real sensor limitation, not a bug.
