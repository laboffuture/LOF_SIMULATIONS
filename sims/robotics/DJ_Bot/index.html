HEARTBEAT DJ BOT — CREW CALMNESS MUSIC SIMULATION

  1. Pick a crew situation. It presets the heart rate and the room noise — every slider stays live afterwards, so you can take over at any moment.
  2. Drag Heart Rate up. Watch the PPG wave beat faster, the detected BPM climb past the threshold line, and the bot switch to a calming track.
  3. Change the sound type and push Ambient Level up. Each type has its own signature — an alarm spikes, machinery hums flat, a crowd is dense and random.
  4. Watch the situation matrix on the right. The two thresholds split the world into four boxes, and the lit box is the bot's decision. Nothing is random.
  5. Move the threshold sliders. Put one right on top of the current reading and the bot starts flapping between modes — that is the "too sensitive" lesson.
  6. Turn Sensor Averaging down to 1 sample: the BPM reading goes jumpy. Turn it up to 16: smooth, but it reacts late.
  7. Leave it running. Calming music pulls the heart rate down, the bot sees it falling, and Recovery Mode appears on its own.
