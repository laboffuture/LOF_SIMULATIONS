Aero Build Flight Manual

Motor-Powered Rocket Design & Stability

Everything the simulation needs comes from the design you build and the motor designation you type in — no outside data lookups.

  1. Design: Pick a nose shape, size the body, add fins, and drag the CG marker onto the rocket. The stability gauge reacts instantly.
  2. Static Margin: (CP − CG) / diameter, in body-diameters ("calibers"). 1–2 calibers is the classic stable range; below 1 the rocket can tumble.
  3. Checklist & Launch: Commit opens a checklist — margin, mass, motor, and moment of inertia — then launches into the Flight view.
  4. Wind: A stable rocket weathercocks — it pitches into the wind — and still drifts downwind. Watch the pitch graph.
  5. Recovery: The parachute opens using the motor's real ejection delay (or, in Dual Deploy, a small drogue at apogee and the main chute at a backup altitude). Safe descent is 4.5–6 m/s.
