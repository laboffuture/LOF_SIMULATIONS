Aerodynamics across Solar System Atmospheres

Rotorcraft generate aerodynamic lift by spinning blades to push atmospheric gas downwards. Lift force depends on atmospheric density (ρ), rotor radius (R), and rotation speed (ω).

  1. Earth: Standard atmosphere (ρ = 1.225 kg/m³) and gravity (9.81 m/s²).
  2. Mars: Extremely thin air (ρ = 0.016 kg/m³). High-speed rotors (Ingenuity mission) required to achieve lift.
  3. Titan: Dense atmosphere (ρ = 5.2 kg/m³) and low gravity (1.35 m/s²). Ideal environment for heavy aerial explorers (Dragonfly mission).
  4. Moon: Vacuum environment (ρ = 0). Rotorcraft cannot fly without an atmosphere to push against.
