Astro Build

Catch the modules · dock them by shape · then save the station from memory

Modules fall one at a time. Nothing is labelled — no names, no functions, no hints. All you get is the shape of the module and the pattern of its docking ports, and a blueprint of empty berths waiting to be filled.

When the station is finished the emergencies start, and they never name the module you need. "CO₂ is rising in the sleeping quarters" — you have to already know which box on the station handles that, from having built it yourself.

How To Play

Two phases, one skill
  1. Build. A module drifts down from the top. Tap the berth on the blueprint where it belongs before it falls off screen. Match the outline and the docking-port marks on the berth to the module in your hand.
  2. Wrong berth — it bounces back into the queue and you take a strike. Three strikes and one system enters the crisis phase already in the warning zone.
  3. Crisis. Situations arrive on a countdown and never name the module. Tap the module on the station that handles it.
  4. Wrong tap — the crisis escalates and its countdown accelerates. Let a crisis expire and its system enters warning. Three systems in warning and the station is lost.
  5. Scoring. First-try placements, fast crisis solves, and triaging the most urgent crisis first all earn more. Never enter warning at all for a perfect run.

Every module here is a real ISS component in a real berth. The end screen names all of them — but only after you have flown it.
