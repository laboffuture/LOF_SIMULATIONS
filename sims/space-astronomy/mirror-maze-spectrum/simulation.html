Mirror Maze Spectrum

A light workbench. Nothing to win — just try things.

The top view is the bench: light leaves the source on the left and travels right through up to two objects you place. The bottom view is the screen — it shows exactly what the light looks like when it lands.

  1. Try this: drag the Prism into Slot 1 and keep White light on. Watch a rainbow spread on the screen.
  2. Now flip to One color — the rainbow collapses to a single dot. A prism can only spread colors that are already there.
  3. Add a Mirror in Slot 1 and a Prism in Slot 2. Turn off auto-aim and drag the screen to catch the bent beam.
Wavelength
A number (in nanometres, nm) that says which color a light is. About 400 = violet, 550 = green, 700 = red.
Reflect (Mirror)
The light bounces off and changes direction. The bounce angle equals the arrival angle. All colors bounce the same.
Refract / Disperse (Prism)
The light bends as it passes through glass. Each color bends a little differently, so white light fans out into a rainbow.
Snell's Law
The rule that tells you exactly how much light bends: 1.00 × sin(in) = n × sin(out). The prism shows it live.
Diffraction (Grating)
A surface with thousands of tiny lines that also splits light — but the long-wave red spreads out most here, the opposite order from a prism.
Filter
A colored window that lets only its own band of colors through and soaks up the rest.
Diffuser
Frosted glass that scatters the beam into a soft fuzzy glow.
Lens
Curved glass that pulls the beam together (converging) into a tight spot, or spreads it out (diverging).
