Anemometer

  1. Pick a Wind Speed — Off, Low, Medium or High.
  2. Choose where the wind comes from with Wind Direction — Front, Left or Right — and pick a Rotor Size.
  3. Press Start to switch the wind on. Watch the cups spin up; the Turns counter under the readings counts every full turn.
  4. Read the RPM meter, the Rotor Speed and the measured Wind Speed in m/s and km/h.
  5. Open the Rotation → Wind Speed box to see exactly how the RPM is turned into a wind speed.
  6. Turn Wind Gust ON and watch the RPM jump each time a gust arrives. The graph shows every gust.
  7. Change one setting at a time — speed, direction or rotor size — and compare the graph and readings.
  8. Press Reset to stop the wind and clear the graph.
