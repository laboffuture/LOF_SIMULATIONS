1. Set the Flight Conditions: pick a Preset (ready-made situations that use every part of the simulation), or build your own from the Route, Time of departure, Ground Ice and Weather.
  2. Fly between London, Berlin, Moscow, Tokyo and Delhi. Each airport uses a real runway and has its real surroundings; each city's skyline sits in its real direction from the airport. The arrival is at the destination's real local time.
  3. Only realistic conditions can be chosen: each city only offers the weather it really gets (Delhi never freezes, Tokyo has no freezing rain), and ice can only be on the aircraft when it is below freezing.
  4. The weather changes the flight: where the icing cloud is (ice only forms in cloud or freezing rain, between about 0 °C and −20 °C), how fast ice builds in it, how rough the air is, and how slippery the runway is.
  5. Press Inspect Aircraft. The Inspection list shows the ice on the aircraft, the outside air and the moisture.
  6. Choose on the Ice Protection System panel: De-Ice (two boom trucks spray hot Type I fluid, then green Type IV fluid if it is still snowing) or No Action. Anti-Ice is built into the aircraft: it can be switched on once the engines run (engine anti-ice at once, the wing anti-ice after lift-off).
  7. Watch the take-off and flight on the Flight Instruments and the Live Position map. When ice starts forming in the icing cloud, a pop-up warns you, the Ice Protection System panel lights up and a timer starts — act before it runs out, or the crew declares MAYDAY, turns back to the nearest suitable airport and the flight is not completed. Once out of the icing, a second pop-up offers to switch the anti-ice off.
  8. Read the result and the explanation. If a decision was wrong, press Try Again; if it was right, go on to the Next Preset. Fast Forward speeds up the calm parts of the flight.
  • Instead of directly telling you which system to use, the simulation gives you a flight situation. You inspect the condition, think, and act.
  • Flow: Flight Conditions → Inspect Aircraft/Weather → Choose an Action → See System Action → Take-off & Flight → Safe Flight or Failure → Explanation
