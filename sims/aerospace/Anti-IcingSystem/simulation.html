1. Pick a mission (01–05) and read the Mission Brief. The aircraft is parked on the airport apron, waiting for take-off.
  2. Set the Weather if you want to try a different situation: Clear, Cold & Dry, Snow, Freezing Fog or Freezing Rain. Each mission starts with its own weather.
  3. Press Inspect Aircraft. A scanner checks the aircraft, and the Inspection list shows the ice on the aircraft, the outside air and the moisture.
  4. Think, then choose: De-Icing (remove ice that has already formed), Anti-Icing (prevent ice from forming) or No Action / Monitor (use when icing conditions are not present).
  5. Watch the ground crew trucks spray orange de-icing fluid or green anti-icing fluid. Then the engines start and the aircraft taxis to the runway.
  6. Watch the take-off and flight to see what your decision does: a normal flight and a safe landing, a rejected take-off with an iced wing, or ice building up in the cloud until the mission is aborted and the aircraft returns. Press Skip to result to jump to the end.
  7. If the ice is removed but icing weather continues, you will be asked to choose again.
  8. Read the result — Landed Safely, Take-off Not Safe, Mission Aborted or Unnecessary Energy Use — and the explanation. The Wing Section view shows whether the airflow is smooth or disturbed.
  9. If the decision was wrong, press Try Again. If it was right, press Next Mission.
  • Instead of directly telling you which system to use, the simulation gives you a flight situation. You inspect the condition, think, and choose: De-Icing / Anti-Icing / No Action.
  • Flow: Mission Brief → Inspect Aircraft/Weather → Choose Protection Method → See System Action → Safe Mission or Failure → Explanation
  • Core learning — discover through the scenarios:
    Ice already existsDE-ICE
    Ice may form soonANTI-ICE
    Ice removed but icing conditions continueANTI-ICE afterwards
    Cold air alone does not always mean icingmoisture is also important
  • This makes the simulation a decision-making mission, rather than simply an ON/OFF heater demonstration.
