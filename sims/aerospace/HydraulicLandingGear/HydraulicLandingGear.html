Hydraulic Landing Gear

  1. Choose the Aircraft Phase. On Landing the gear starts up and locked; on Take-off it starts down and locked.
  2. Set the Hydraulic Pressure (Low, Medium or High) and the Load Condition (Light or Heavy).
  3. Press Start to switch the hydraulic pump on.
  4. Use the Gear Command lever — Extend to lower the gear, Retract to raise it. You can flip it again at any time.
  5. Watch the fluid flow arrows, the piston in the cylinder and the gear leg moving. The bottom diagram shows where the fluid goes.
  6. Check the Lock Status and the cockpit lights: green means down and locked, red means the gear is moving or unlocked.
  7. Try Heavy load with Low pressure, then raise the pressure while it's stuck and see what happens.
  8. Press Reset to switch the pump off and put the gear back to its starting position.
