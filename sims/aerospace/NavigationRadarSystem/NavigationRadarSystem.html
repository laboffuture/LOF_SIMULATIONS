1. Press Start Flight. The aircraft flies forward and the radar starts sweeping its 0°–180° front arc.
  2. Watch the Side View (altitude) and the Top View (left and right) together. An obstacle is only in your way if it lines up in both views.
  3. Hold Climb, Descend, Left or Right, or use the arrow keys or W A S D, to steer around mountains, aircraft, masts, balloons, drones and birds. If you touch one, or the sea, the flight ends.
  4. Each object the radar detects becomes a tracked contact. Its blip updates every time the beam passes over it. Press + Log to add a contact to the Detection Log.
  5. Switch Visibility to Fog to hide obstacles in the views, so you have to fly by radar alone.
  6. Narrow the Scan Angle or turn the radar off to see what the radar stops noticing.
  • Radar finds an object's direction from the angle where the sweep line meets it
  • Radar finds distance from how far out along that angle the echo returns
  • Near contacts are a red danger warning, medium contacts a yellow caution, and far contacts a green safe reading
  • Radar reaches further than you can see, and it sees through fog, so it gives you time to react
  • A narrower scan angle covers less of the arc, and anything in the blind zone is never detected
  • A faster sweep revisits each spot more often, so tracked blips update more quickly
  • To avoid an obstacle you need its direction, distance and height, then you climb, descend or steer away
