1. Pick a Blower / Wind Speed — Low, Medium or High. Nothing spins until you choose one.
  2. Choose the Number of Blades — 2, 3 or 4 — and read the note under it.
  3. Pick a Load Device — LED, Battery or Small Gadget — for the generator to power.
  4. Press Start and watch the rotor spin up.
  5. Read Live Readings — turbine RPM, generator voltage, load current, and how the device is doing: an LED reports Off, Dim, On or Bright, while a battery or gadget reports its charging status.
  6. Watch the Energy Flow strip light up: wind → turbine → generator → device.
  7. Count the cuts across the rotor to read the blade setting — 3 blades is one body cut twice. The Top View inset shows the curved cross-section those blades are built from.
  8. Press Reset, change one setting, and run again to compare.
