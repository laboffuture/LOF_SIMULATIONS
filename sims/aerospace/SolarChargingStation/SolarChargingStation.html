1. Pick Day or Night — solar power only exists by day.
  2. By day, set the Sunlight Intensity and drag the Panel Angle slider — try to land near the marked sweet spot.
  3. Choose the Device Load the station is powering.
  4. By Day, the battery charges from the panel and powers the device at the same time. By Night there's no sunlight, so only the device draws from what's stored.
  5. Press Start and watch the readings and energy flow.
  6. Try this: let the battery build up during the Day, then switch to Night — the device keeps running on what you stored, with no panel left to top it back up.
  7. Press Reset to clear the battery and start over.
  • Solar power is only generated when sunlight is available
  • Stronger sunlight and a correctly angled panel generate more power
  • A wrongly angled panel captures far less usable sunlight
  • Stored battery energy can be used anytime, including at night
  • A heavier device load drains a battery faster
  • By day a charging station can charge its battery and power a device at the same time; by night it can only draw from what's stored
  • How energy changes form: sunlight → electrical energy → stored energy → device power
