What you are testing

A crew capsule is returning from orbit. You choose its size, how much it carries, its parachute, the height the parachute opens, and the wind. Then you fly the return and see whether it survives the water landing.

How it works

  • The capsule hits the air at 7.8 km/s heat-shield-down. The heat shield bleeds off almost all of that speed.
  • The parachute opens at your chosen height. Its drag slows the fall to a steady terminal speed that depends on total mass ÷ parachute area.
  • Wind pushes the capsule sideways the whole way down — slow descents drift furthest.

Reading the result

  • Safe ≤ 10 m/s  ·  Hard 10–20 m/s  ·  Failed > 20 m/s
  • Mission Success needs a Safe splashdown inside the target zone.

Try this

  • Fly the Heavy capsule on the Small chute, then swap to Large.
  • Add 3 astronauts and watch total mass drive the splashdown speed up.
  • Drop the deploy height to Low with a heavy load — the chute opens too late.
  • Put the Light capsule on the Large chute in Strong wind and watch it drift off target.
